About Prakash Pharma

Delivering Genuine Medicines With Trust & Quality

Prakash Pharma is one of the trusted pharmaceutical distributors located in Ranebennur, Karnataka. We are committed to providing authentic medicines, healthcare products, surgical supplies, and pharmaceutical solutions to hospitals, pharmacies, clinics, and healthcare professionals across the region.

Who We Are

Prakash Pharma has established itself as a dependable pharmaceutical distributor known for supplying genuine medicines from India's leading pharmaceutical companies.

With years of experience in pharmaceutical distribution, our organization focuses on quality, transparency, timely delivery, and long-term customer relationships.

We supply medicines to retail pharmacies, medical stores, hospitals, healthcare institutions, and distributors while ensuring compliance with industry standards and proper storage practices.
